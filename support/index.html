Penguin Pair

Support

Need help with Penguin Pair? Send a message to support@penguinpair.app.

Common Help Topics

  • For subscription or billing issues, manage your subscription through your Apple ID subscription settings.
  • For account, pairing, prompt, or memory issues, email support with the email address used for your Penguin Pair account.
  • For account or data deletion requests, email support and include "Data deletion request" in the subject line.

Response Time

We try to respond to support requests as soon as possible.
