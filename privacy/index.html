Penguin Pair

Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 14, 2026

Penguin Pair helps couples answer prompts, save shared memories, and build a private relationship ritual together. This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, and the choices you have.

Information We Collect

We may collect the following information when you use Penguin Pair:

  • Account information, such as your email address and authentication identifiers.
  • Profile and onboarding information you choose to provide, such as your name, relationship context, prompt preferences, and app personalization choices.
  • Pair information, such as invite codes, partner connection status, shared progress, streaks, and app activity related to your pair.
  • Content you create in the app, including prompt answers, saved memories, selected photos, and related metadata.
  • Purchase and entitlement information provided by RevenueCat and Apple, such as subscription status, product identifiers, renewal status, and transaction metadata.
  • Usage and diagnostic information, such as app events, device type, build version, crash or error details, and analytics events.

How We Use Information

We use information to:

  • Provide and operate Penguin Pair.
  • Connect you with your partner and keep your shared experience in sync.
  • Save and display your prompts, memories, streaks, rewards, and settings.
  • Process subscriptions and manage access to paid features.
  • Send app notifications when enabled.
  • Understand app usage, troubleshoot issues, prevent abuse, and improve the product.
  • Respond to support requests.

Service Providers

Penguin Pair uses third-party service providers to run the app. These may include Supabase for authentication, database, storage, and backend functions; RevenueCat for subscription management; Apple for App Store purchases; Expo for app builds, updates, and notifications; and PostHog for product analytics.

Sharing

We do not sell your personal information. We share information only as needed to operate the app, comply with law, protect rights and safety, process subscriptions, provide support, or with your direction, such as when you connect with your partner inside the app.

Your Choices

You can choose what content to add to Penguin Pair. You can manage notification permissions in your device settings. You can manage subscriptions through your Apple ID subscription settings. You may request account or data deletion by contacting us at support@penguinpair.app.

Data Retention

We keep information for as long as needed to provide Penguin Pair, comply with legal obligations, resolve disputes, enforce agreements, and maintain security. If you request deletion, we will delete or de-identify information unless we need to retain it for legal, security, or operational reasons.

Children

Penguin Pair is not intended for children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

Security

We use reasonable administrative, technical, and organizational measures to protect information. No method of storage or transmission is completely secure.

Changes

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. The updated version will be posted on this page with a new "Last updated" date.

Contact

Questions or requests can be sent to support@penguinpair.app.
